# Terminology

As Stained Mists is a game based in the Mistborn world, a lot of the terminology is inherited from it. You do not need to know all of it, but knowing some basic words will help with understanding the game.

</br>

## Mistborn terminology

**Allomancy** — The magic system of the game. It works by ingesting certain metals, then "burning" them to use their powers.

**Misting** — A person who can burn an allomantic metal.

**Mistborn** — A person who can burn all allomantic metals.

## Specific classes (you do not need to know these)

**Coinshot** — A misting who can burn Steel.

**Lurcher** — A misting who can burn Iron.

**Thug** — A misting who can burn Pewter.

**Tineye** — A misting who can burn Tin.

**Seeker** — A misting who can burn Bronze.

**Smoker** — A misting who can burn Copper.

**Soother** — A misting who can burn Brass.

**Rioter** — A misting who can burn Zinc.


</br>

## Stained Mists terminology

**Master Skill** — A skill which can be unlocked at max level, and (usually) cannot be unlocked by a Mistborn.

**Deep Master Skill** — A master skill which has another master skill as its requirement. It can _never_ be unlocked by a Mistborn.

**Original Mistborn** — A full-power Mistborn who has consumed Lerasium. They have reduced fuel usage, special night idle VFX, and the ability to unlock _one_ master skill.
